01 / THE WORKSHOP

Same curiosity.
A safer tomorrow.

Step into the world of cybersecurity with ACM Student Chapter, VIT Chennai. This five-day online workshop brings together industry professionals and academic expertise to explore how we protect systems, data, and people.

From Linux and Zero Trust to AI security and penetration testing, get a glimpse of the challenges defenders face—and put your learning into practice with a daily Capture the Flag (CTF) challenge.

Learn in the sessions. Think like a defender in the CTF.
