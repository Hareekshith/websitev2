TECHNOVIT COLLABORATION // SEP 04, 2026

Prompt to Prototype

"Turn Prompts into Working Prototypes with Google's Antigravity"

9:30 AM – 1:30 PM AB1 – 401 Ajoe Joseph (Google)
← Back to Events

Event Overview

The ACM Student Chapter, VIT Chennai in prestigious collaboration with TechnoVIT organized a transformative hands-on technical workshop titled “Prompt to Prototype” on September 4, 2026, at AB1 – 401. The session brought together passionate developers, designers, and innovators keen to explore the bleeding edge of AI-assisted software engineering and agentic app generation.

Speaker & Resource Person

The session was spearheaded by Ajoe Joseph — Software Engineer & Community Lead at Code Vipassana by Google. With his deep expertise in developer tooling and AI ecosystems, Ajoe guided participants through foundational principles of prompt engineering, iterative refinement, and building production-ready prototypes rapidly using Google’s latest developer technologies.

Key Highlights & Takeaways

  • Exploring Google’s Antigravity Tool: Deep-dive into next-generation AI-assisted development paradigms and multi-agent systems.
  • Turning Prompts into Prototypes: Structuring context, system prompts, and architectural constraints to generate full-stack web applications in minutes.
  • Hands-on Tech Experience: Interactive live coding sprints where participants implemented and deployed functional prototypes on the spot.
  • Modern AI Toolchains: Leveraging cutting-edge AI engines to automate boilerplate code, unit testing, and UI/UX design.

Event Proceedings

Opening & Introduction: The workshop kicked off at 9:30 AM with an address on how developer roles are evolving from manual coding to intelligent orchestration and rapid prototyping.

Live Walkthrough & Demonstration: Ajoe Joseph demonstrated live prototyping workflows using Google's Antigravity, showcasing how abstract concepts transform seamlessly into functional full-stack interfaces.

Hands-on Rapid Prototyping Sprint: Participants were tasked with formulating prompts and creating interactive prototypes across multiple domains including healthcare, education, productivity, and sustainability.

Project Pitch & Evaluation: Teams submitted their working prototypes, which were evaluated based on architectural soundness, UI responsiveness, user experience, and prompt design efficacy.
