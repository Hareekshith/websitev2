TECHNOVIT COLLABORATION // SEP 01, 2026

Flutterverse

"Turn Ideas into Functional Mobile Apps with Flutter & Dart"

9:30 AM – 1:30 PM AB4 – 419 Free ACM Membership
← Back to Events

Event Overview

The ACM Student Chapter, VIT Chennai in dynamic collaboration with TechnoVIT organized an intensive, hands-on app development workshop titled “FLUTTERVERSE” on September 1, 2026, held at AB4 – 419 from 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM. Designed to empower participants to transition from passive smartphone users to active creators, the workshop provided a complete zero-to-one guide to building modern cross-platform mobile applications.

Organizing Team

The workshop was organized and executed under the mentorship of Faculty Coordinator Dr. Subbulakshmi T and led by Student Coordinators Adithiaya Murugan K S and Faheem Abdullah A. All registered participants were also awarded an exclusive complimentary ACM Student Chapter Membership.

Key Highlights & Objectives

  • Exploring Flutter & Dart: Understanding widget architectures, reactive state handling, and the Dart language foundation.
  • Cross-Platform Development: Creating sleek, responsive mobile UIs that run natively on both Android and iOS from a single codebase.
  • Hands-on Coding Experience: Interactive live coding sessions guiding every attendee from their first line of code to testing on real hardware.
  • Building Your Own App: By the end of the 4-hour immersive session, each participant engineered and launched their very own custom mobile app.

Event Proceedings

Foundations & Environment Setup: The workshop commenced with establishing Flutter dev environments, configuring Flutter SDK, Android Studio/VS Code emulators, and exploring Dart syntax essentials.

Widget Trees & UI Architecture: Attendees explored stateless vs. stateful widgets, container layouts, animations, navigation routing, and responsive mobile design patterns.

Live App Building Sprint: Guided by the coordinators and mentors, participants implemented real-time state management, API integration, and user interactivity to craft functional mobile applications.

Testing, Device Deployment & Showcase: Participants debugged and compiled their apps directly onto physical mobile devices, demonstrating their creations during the final showcase sprint.
