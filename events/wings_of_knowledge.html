LITERARY INITIATIVE // AUG 23, 2026

ACM Wings of Knowledge

"Read. Reflect. Recommend."

11:00 AM - 12:00 PM Online / Virtual Session
← Back to Events

Event Overview

The ACM Student Chapter, VIT Chennai, in collaboration with the Office of Students’ Welfare, successfully organized an online literary event titled “ACM Wings of Knowledge” on August 23, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 12:00 Noon. The event was conducted with the theme “Read. Reflect. Recommend.”, encouraging students to explore books, reflect on their ideas, and share meaningful recommendations with others under the guidance of Faculty Coordinator Dr. T. Subbulakshmi and Student Coordinators Snigdha Neya Baskar and Ranjana.

Event Objectives

The event aimed to promote reading and meaningful discussions among students by creating a platform where books could be explored beyond simply reading them. It focused on encouraging participants to connect the lessons and ideas from books with real-life experiences and personal growth.

The event particularly sought individuals who could:

  • Host engaging sessions where books are experienced and applied rather than merely read.
  • Share thoughtful insights and turn lessons from books into meaningful conversations.
  • Lead discussions exploring themes such as purpose, resilience, and transformation.

Event Proceedings

Welcome and Introduction: The event commenced with a welcome to the participants, followed by an introduction to the purpose and theme of ACM Wings of Knowledge.

Reading and Reflection: The session focused on the importance of reading with understanding and reflecting on the ideas and lessons presented through books.

Sharing of Insights: Participants were encouraged to share their thoughts, perspectives, and recommendations, creating an interactive environment around books and their practical relevance.

Discussion and Interaction: The event provided an opportunity for participants to engage in discussions on themes such as purpose, resilience, and personal transformation, highlighting how books can influence everyday life.

Conclusion

The ACM Wings of Knowledge event provided an engaging platform for students to develop a deeper appreciation for reading and meaningful discussions. Through the theme “Read. Reflect. Recommend.”, the event emphasized that books can serve not only as sources of knowledge but also as tools for reflection, personal growth, and transformation. The initiative successfully encouraged participants to share their perspectives and inspire others through the power of reading.
