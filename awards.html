The academic year 2025–2026 was a defining milestone for the ACM Student Chapter at VIT Chennai. Operating with a relentless commitment to technical excellence and community-driven learning, our chapter rose above 130+ active clubs to be crowned the Best Technical Chapter on campus.

From high-capacity coding competitions like CodePulse and Server Surfers CTF to foundational bootcamps in Data Structures and Algorithms, we engaged over 1,900 participants across 45+ institutions nationwide, cementing our standing in the prestigious Asia Book of Records.

A look back at the defining milestones of our award-winning year
Outreach & Inception

Computer Literacy & Sustain-A-Thon

Our journey began with giving back—introducing computing concepts in a rural school, followed by our foundational sustainability hackathon.

Skill Acceleration

TechJam, Webdex & Frontend Kickstart

Foundational technical tracks spanning Machine Learning, Cloud architectures, modern frontend development, and competitive logic.

Flagship Tournaments

CodePulse & ACM Club Expo

Fostering algorithmic problem solving before welcoming incoming students with interactive engineering games and live tech demonstrations.

Systems Foundations

Linux & Git / Pages to Progress

Empowering 340+ developers with open-source fundamentals, command-line mastery, and Git version control workflows.
