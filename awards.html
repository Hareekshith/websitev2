The academic year 2025–2026 was a defining milestone for the ACM Student Chapter at VIT Chennai. Operating with a relentless commitment to technical excellence and community-driven learning, our chapter rose above 130+ active clubs to be crowned the Best Technical Chapter on campus.

From highly anticipated events like CodePulse and Server Surfers CTF to foundational bootcamps in Data Structures and Algorithms, we engaged over 1,900 participants across 45+ institutions nationwide, cementing our standing in the prestigious Asia Book of Records.

The Road to Excellence

A look back at the defining milestones of our award-winning year
The Inception & Outreach

Computer Literacy & Sustain-A-Thon

Our journey began with giving back—introducing essential computer concepts in a village school, followed by our foundational sustainability hackathon.

Skill Building & Logic

TechJam, Webdex & Frontend Kickstart

A rapid succession of foundational events focusing on ML, Cloud, Ethical Hacking, modern frontend development, and intense problem-solving in Rubik's Rumble.

Summer Milestones

CodePulse & ACM Club Expo

Fostering algorithmic problem-solving before welcoming the incoming batch of students with interactive coding games and live technical demonstrations.

Laying the Foundation

Linux & Git / Pages to Progress

Empowering developers with open-source fundamentals and version control, alongside inspiring young minds through the art of book reviews and critical reading.

Cybersecurity & Global Exposure

Cybermonth & ACM India Visit

An intense period of ethical hacking challenges in our Value Added Program, followed by global research interaction and networking with ACM India leadership.

Next-Gen Technologies

Gen AI & Docker Showdown

Exploring the Agentic Shift and autonomous systems with Google's Gemini, followed by a massive networking and knowledge exchange around software deployment and DevOps.

Life Skills & Community Impact

Personal Finance & Hour of AI

Bridging the gap in student financial literacy, and reaching beyond our campus to introduce school students to the future of AI Game Development.

Breaking Records

DSA Bootcamp & CodePulse March

A month-long national DSA bootcamp combining expert-led training with a high-stakes algorithmic challenge, testing logic and skills under immense pressure.

The Grand Finale

Server Surfers CTF

Concluding our award-winning run with a massive Capture The Flag cybersecurity challenge, featuring industry keynote insights from Kaspersky.
